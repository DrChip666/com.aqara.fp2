The Aqara FP2 does more than notice movement: its radar sees people sitting still, reading on the sofa or working at a desk, and it can split a room into zones. This app brings that precision straight into Homey, so lights stay on while you are in the room and Flows can react to exactly where someone is.

Everything runs locally on your own network, without a cloud in between. Each zone you draw in the Aqara Home app shows up in Homey with its own presence status, together with the room's light level.
